# Rules for using this tool on Wikipedia

Whether this project works depends on how its output is used, not only on the
code. Breaking these rules can get accounts sanctioned and the project
discredited, whatever the quality of the findings.

## The topic area is under special restrictions

Articles on the Arab–Israeli conflict are a designated *contentious topic*
(WP:ARBPIA, WP:CT/A-I). In practice:

- Only **extended-confirmed** accounts (at least 500 edits and 30 days old) can
  edit these articles or take part in most discussions about them. Other
  accounts can only post **edit requests** on the talk page.
- Administrators can sanction editors without a long process. The Arbitration
  Committee has sanctioned coordinated editing groups on both sides.

Check the current rules on Wikipedia before acting. They change.

## How findings reach Wikipedia

1. **A person posts, from their own account, using their own judgement.** The
   tool never posts and never edits.
2. **Re-check the source yourself** before posting. The tool's verdict is a lead,
   not evidence.
3. **Rewrite the draft in your own words.** Talk-page comments that look
   AI-generated are increasingly discounted or collapsed.
4. **Say you used a tool**, e.g. "I found this with a citation-checking tool and
   then checked the source myself."
5. **One issue per thread, a few at a time.** Wait for replies. Many posts in a
   short time look like a campaign, and editors will treat them as one.
6. **No coordination.** Don't share findings as "action items" or ask others to
   join a discussion or support an edit. That is meatpuppetry / canvassing
   (WP:MEAT, WP:CANVASS), and it is the fastest way to get the project banned.
7. **Disclose conflicts of interest.** If anyone is paid or acting for an
   organisation, follow WP:COI and WP:PAID.

## Where each kind of finding goes

| Finding | Where |
|---|---|
| Cited source doesn't support the text (F2) | Article talk page, or `{{edit request}}` |
| Questionable source (F3, later phase) | Check WP:RSP first, then WP:RSN |
| Contested view stated as fact (F5, later phase) | Talk page, then WP:NPOVN |
| Anything about living people | WP:BLPN |

## Content, not editors

The tool looks at article content and sources. It does not profile, track or
report on individual editors. Revision history is used only to show what text
changed and when. Don't build features that identify or follow editors
(WP:HOUNDING, WP:OUTING).

## Even-handedness

Apply the same standard whichever side a claim favours. Track flag rates per
perspective (see `wcm eval` and the Stats page), and publish the method. If the
tool flags only one side's claims, the findings will not be taken seriously, and
they may not be right.
